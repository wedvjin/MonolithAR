MONOLITHAR

Clash of Monoliths — a multiplayer arena in your room

How to play
  • Anchor the arena on your floor (AR) — everyone shares the same battlefield, each in their own room.
  • Walk into the glowing wisps to harvest energy.
  • Tap / click / trigger to fire plasma bolts at rival monoliths (10⚡ per shot). On Quest, aim with the controller laser.
  • Hold shield (🛡 button, F key, or grip on Quest) to protect your monolith — it burns energy fast.
  • Shooting a rival player steals their energy.
  • Last monolith standing — or healthiest after 3 minutes — wins.
